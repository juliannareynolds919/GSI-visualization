About this visualization

This interactive dashboard accompanies a systematic literature review of green stormwater infrastructure (GSI) research in arid and semi-arid climates, conducted as a Master of Science capstone project in Geographic Information Systems Technology at the University of Arizona (2025). This work was supported by the Arizona Tri-University Recharge and Water Reliability Project (ATUR).

Credits Research: Julianna Reynolds

Full report — University of Arizona Campus Repository
How to use
  • Relationship Explorer — hover over any node to highlight its connections. Papers connect to their location, methodology, GSI practice, and results.
  • Study Locations — hover over a map marker to highlight the corresponding paper in the chart. Click a marker to see paper details including a DOI link.
  • Drag the center divider to resize the two panels.
